Voyager 1

Launched from Cape Canaveral atop a Titan IIIE-Centaur rocket into a hyperbolic solar escape trajectory, Voyager 1 embarked on humanity's grandest voyage. Engineered with a 3.7-meter parabolic dish and triply-redundant onboard computers, it was built to endure where no machine had ever ventured.

Chapter 02 — 05 March 1979

The Grand Tour

Reaching Jupiter in March 1979, Voyager 1 threaded the needle of the giant planet's immense gravitational well. Through the historic slingshot maneuver, it stole angular momentum from Jupiter's orbital path, hurtling outward toward the ringed jewel of the solar system.

Chapter 03 — 12 November 1980

Saturnian Gateway

Voyager 1 made its closest approach to Saturn in November 1980, revealing thousands of intricate ringlets and sampling the nitrogen-rich atmosphere of giant moon Titan. Titan's gravity deflected the spacecraft northward, out of the ecliptic plane, on a permanent course toward the galaxy's rim.

Chapter 04 — Humanity's Time Capsule

The Golden Record

Mounted securely on the probe's side bus sits a 12-inch gold-plated copper phonograph record. Curated by Carl Sagan, it holds 115 analog images, greetings in 55 languages, 90 minutes of global music, and the recorded brainwaves of Ann Druyan thinking of love.

Chapter 05 — 25 August 2012 & Beyond

Interstellar Horizon

On August 25, 2012, at a distance of 121 AU, Voyager 1 measured a cliff-edge plunge in solar wind ions and an unprecedented surge in galactic cosmic rays. It had officially crossed the heliopause into the pristine interstellar medium of the Milky Way.
