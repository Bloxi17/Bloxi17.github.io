The Grand Tour

Voyager utilized a rare planetary alignment occurring once every 176 years. By using Jupiter's immense gravity, it slingshotted itself outward, gaining the velocity needed to escape the solar system.

The Golden Record

Attached to the side of the probe is a phonograph record containing sounds and images selected to portray the diversity of life and culture on Earth, intended for any intelligent extraterrestrial life.

Interstellar Space
